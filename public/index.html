About Us

DecorFind connects wholesale buyers across the U.S. with home decor makers and small sellers. Using the Google Places API to find real boutiques, gift shops and interior studios that match your products so you pitch stores that might actually say yes instead of cold calling blindly. Find them. Get their contact. Pitch them. That's all.

How It Works

  • 1. Search

    Tell us your product and state. We search for thousands of US stores, boutiques and design studios via the Google Places API.

  • 2. Connect

    Each buyer card shows their address, website and rating. Grab their contact email from their site and enter it in one click.

  • 3. Send a pitch

    Hit "Send Pitch" and our email API delivers your wholesale inquiry straight to the buyer's inbox. Done.
